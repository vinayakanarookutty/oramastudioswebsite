Project overview

EcoState provides commercial energy solutions across Australia — LED lighting retrofits, solar installations, heat pumps and HVAC upgrades. They specialise in projects eligible for government energy-upgrade rebates (VEU/VEET) to reduce operating costs for businesses.

Orama Studios modernised EcoState’s website to: clarify rebate eligibility, present services as conversion-focused pages, and streamline enquiries so commercial prospects can request fast, accurate quotes and site assessments.

Client challenges & requirements

The client needed a clean, trust-building site that explained complex rebate rules simply and converted visitors into qualified leads.

  • Clear explanation for VEU/VEET eligibility and next steps
  • Service pages for LED, solar and heat pump solutions
  • Fast load times and SEO-friendly content to attract local commercial traffic
  • Simple contact/quote flows for site assessment bookings

Project solution

We implemented a lightweight, responsive layout with dedicated service modules and clear CTAs. Each service page contains eligibility notes, benefits, and an enquiry form that feeds leads into EcoState’s process for fast assessment and quoting.
